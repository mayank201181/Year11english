# Year 11 IGCSE English Language (`9270`) & Literature (`4ET1`) Lab

Interactive zero-login study guide, question bank, mock exam hub, interactive explorables, procedural fluency drills, and narrated animated sitcom season built for **Vanishree** (Year 11, Tanglin Trust School).

## Syllabuses & Set Texts Covered
- **IGCSE English Language — OxfordAQA (`9270`)**:
  1. `lang-p1-reading` — Paper 1A: Literary Non-Fiction Close Reading (40 marks)
  2. `lang-p1-composition` — Paper 1B: Descriptive, Narrative & Discursive Composition (40 marks)
  3. `lang-p2-sources` — Paper 2A: Multi-Source Reading & Synthesis (40 marks)
  4. `lang-p2-directed` — Paper 2B: Directed Writing for Audience & Purpose (40 marks)
- **IGCSE English Literature — Pearson Edexcel (`4ET1`)**:
  5. `lit-of-mice-and-men` — Prose: *Of Mice and Men* (John Steinbeck)
  6. `lit-inspector-calls` — Modern Drama: *An Inspector Calls* (J.B. Priestley)
  7. `lit-macbeth` — Literary Heritage: *Macbeth* (William Shakespeare)
  8. `lit-poetry` — Edexcel Poetry Anthology Comparison (all 16 set poems) & Unseen Modern Poetry
